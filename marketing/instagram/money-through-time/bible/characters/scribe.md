# The scribe (fictional)

A temple-household accountant in Uruk. **Fictional** — no individual from this period is known well
enough to portray; his name, age and life are dramatisation.

## Locked so far (T01 shows hands only)
- Adult man, mid-30s to 40s. Hands: weathered, sun-browned olive-brown skin, short nails with clay
  under them, visible tendons, no rings, no jewellery, no tattoos.
- Clothing at frame edge: plain undyed wool wrap (off-white/natural), no sleeves, no stitched hems.
