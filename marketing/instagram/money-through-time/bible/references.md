# Research and reference sources

Two separate questions per image: **can we learn from it** (anything) and **can we feed it to Flow**
(only images we own or that are public-domain / CC0, e.g. The Met Open Access marked "OA Public
Domain"). Museum photos without that mark are for human comparison only and never uploaded.

## Scholarship (facts for narration and the era file)
- Nissen, Damerow & Englund, *Archaic Bookkeeping: Early Writing and Techniques of Economic
  Administration in the Ancient Near East* (University of Chicago Press, 1993).
- Englund, "Texts from the Late Uruk Period", in Bauer, Englund & Krebernik, *Mesopotamien:
  Späturuk-Zeit und Frühdynastische Zeit* (OBO 160/1, 1998).
- Schmandt-Besserat, *Before Writing* (University of Texas Press, 1992) — the token theory; cite as
  an interpretation.
- CDLI — Cuneiform Digital Library Initiative (cdli.earth), proto-cuneiform corpus.
- **User-supplied references: not received yet.** Add them here; they override anything above.
