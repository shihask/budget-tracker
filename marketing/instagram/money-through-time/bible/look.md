# Money Through Time — look

- **Photorealistic historical film reconstruction.** Real people, real materials; never cartoon,
  painterly, "epic fantasy" or museum-diorama.
- **Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps** for the whole episode (Veo's native rate — never retimed to 30).
- Lenses: 35–50 mm for people, macro for objects. Shallow depth of field, slow motivated camera
  moves (push-in, slide), no drone swoops.
- Light: natural only — hard sun, doorway daylight, dust in the air. Warm earth palette: ochre, clay,
  straw, mud-brick, off-white wool. No teal-orange grade.
- Sound: room tone, materials (clay, reed, grain), distant animals and voices without words.
  Narration over it; **no on-screen speech** (we can't verify Sumerian, and lip-sync is a risk).
- Safe areas: top ~250 px and bottom ~420 px sit under Instagram's UI — keep the subject between.
- On screen, always: a place/date card when the place changes, and a small "Dramatised
  reconstruction" tag. Instagram's "AI info" label is set when posting.
- Narration **Malayalam**, subtitles **English**, burned in. A new narrator voice (not Maaman's).
- No emoji, no invented readable inscriptions, no modern text on props.
