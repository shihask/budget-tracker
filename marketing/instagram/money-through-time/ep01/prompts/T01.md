## Prompt v1 (paste as one block)

```
Vertical 9:16 photorealistic historical film reconstruction, shot on a 35mm cinema camera with a macro lens. Southern Mesopotamia, around 3200 BCE.

Extreme close-up, slightly over a man's right shoulder: his weathered, sun-browned olive-skinned hands hold a small palm-sized tablet of unfired clay, pillow-shaped with softly rounded corners and slightly bulging faces, damp grey-brown clay showing fingerprints and fine surface cracks. A few straight lines scored into the surface divide it into rectangular boxes. Inside the boxes are rows of small number impressions of only two simple kinds: shallow D-shaped notches like fingernail marks, and small round circular dents. With the blunt round end of a thin reed stylus he presses one more slanted notch into an empty box; the clay gives softly and a tiny ridge of clay rises at its edge. A second, thicker reed stylus lies beside the tablet.

On the woven reed mat next to him: a coarse thick-walled conical clay bowl with a slanted bevelled rim and rough, straw-flecked surface, filled with hulled barley grain; a small loose spill of barley; a small dark stone cylinder seal lying on its side. The edge of a plain undyed wool wrap rests on his lap.

Inside a room of sun-dried mud brick with rough mud plaster; a doorway screened with tied bundles of reeds. Low morning sunlight slants through the reed screen and lays soft stripes of light across his hands and the tablet; fine dust floats in the light. Warm earthy palette of ochre, clay, straw and off-white wool.

Camera: one slow, steady push-in toward the tablet over the whole shot, shallow depth of field, focus held on the fresh impression. Keep the tablet in the vertical middle of the frame; the top and bottom of the frame are soft out-of-focus wall and mat.

Sound: quiet room ambience, the soft press of reed into wet clay, grain shifting, faint sheep and birds far outside. No music, no voices.

Avoid: wedge-shaped or triangular marks, cuneiform script, letters, symbols or any readable writing, pictures drawn on the tablet, orange fired terracotta, rings or jewellery, metal tools, coins, modern objects, pyramids, ziggurats, columns, Egyptian or Greek styling, on-screen text or captions.
```
