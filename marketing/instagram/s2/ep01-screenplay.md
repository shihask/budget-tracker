# Manu & Maaman S2 · EP01 — Salary എവിടെ ചെലവാക്കണം?

**Lesson (one sentence):** Before spending, split your salary into Needs, Wants and Savings.
**Length:** 60 s after the voice refit (script timing was ~47 s; speech alone is 50.9 s and is never sped up).
The shot list below shows script timing; `ep01.html` holds the refitted cuts (`C`) and word-synced beats.
**Set:** the S2 3D Kerala living room. A low wooden coffee table in front of the sofa holds the props.
**Numbers:** ₹45,000 salary (S1 continuity), every amount labelled **Example**.
**Product:** Budget Strategy is real — presets Balanced 50/30/20, Stable 60/20/20, Growth 45/20/35,
and Custom. The phone shows the real picker (`assets/app-strategy-screen.webm`, demo account).
No balance, transaction or achievement is shown as Manu's.

Arithmetic (on screen): 50% of 45,000 = 22,500 · 30% = 13,500 · 20% = 9,000 · total 45,000.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
Macro close-up: a glossy new-phone box on the table, price tag swinging. Focus racks from the box
to Manu's wide, shining eyes behind it. He's already asking.
