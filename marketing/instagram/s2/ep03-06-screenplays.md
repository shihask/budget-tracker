## Product accuracy notes

Mint must explain two different things:

1. **Savings Goal — Emergency Fund:** Tracks gradual savings contributions towards the target.
2. **Emergency Fund reserve (Settings → Emergency Fund, "Amount reserved and excluded from spendable balance"; dashboard: Emergency Fund · Reserved):** Keeps the designated amount out of Free Money. There is no separate feature called Reserve Amount.

Do not show the reserve setting as a savings progress tracker.

Do not suggest that the entire ₹6,000 repair bill must immediately come from an emergency fund.
