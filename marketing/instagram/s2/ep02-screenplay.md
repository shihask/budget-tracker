# Manu & Maaman S2 · EP02 — Salary കിട്ടിയിട്ട് പൈസ തീർന്നോ?

**Lesson (one sentence):** Bills that come every month are predictable — set their money aside first, before you spend.
**Length:** 57 s, timed to the recorded voice (speech never sped up).
**Set:** the S2 3D Kerala living room and coffee table; a wall calendar right of the window (new, pays off at the end).
**Numbers:** ₹45,000 salary on the 28th (EP01 continuity), every amount labelled **Example**.
**Product:** Commitments are real — the phone shows the real **Bills & Obligations** page
(`assets/app-commitments-screen.png`, demo account) with the same four example bills:
Unpaid ₹15,000 · Monthly ₹15,000 · Rent, Bike EMI, Phone & Internet, Health Insurance with due dates
and Mark Paid. Commitment reminders are a real push (`commitment-reminder`). Nothing is shown as Manu's
real balance; Mint only repeats what the bills page shows.

Arithmetic (on screen):
- Bills: 9,000 + 4,500 + 500 + 1,000 = **15,000**
- Half gone in 3 days: 45,000 − 22,500 = 22,500 left · 22,500 − 15,000 = **7,500** for the next 27 days
  (salary on the 28th, day 3 → the cycle runs to the 27th)
- Bills first: 45,000 − 15,000 = **30,000** to plan
- Table: one coin stack = ₹7,500 — 3 stacks → 1, and 6 stacks → 4; each time 2 stacks go to the bills.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
Manu, wide-eyed, shakes his open wallet upside down at the camera. One lonely coin tumbles out in
comic slow motion; focus racks from the wallet to his face as he wails.
