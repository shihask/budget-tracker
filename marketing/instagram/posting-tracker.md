# Instagram posting tracker — every post and reel we made

**Rule: nothing we create is skipped.** Every finished asset is listed here with a status and a date.
A schedule change moves a post to a later date; it never removes it. New content (e.g. the next
Manu & Maaman episode) is added here the day it's rendered. "Today's post" is always read from
this table.

Status: **Posted** · **Scheduled** (date) · **Not a post** (raw clip, pilot or a shorter version of
something posted — listed so it's never mistaken for a missed post).

All paths are relative to `marketing/instagram/out/`. Captions: `content-plan.md` (batch 1, value
reels, Kakeibo) and `captions.md` (app reels, Manu & Maaman).
