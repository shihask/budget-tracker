**Platform:** Instagram Reels

**Audience:** Malayalam-speaking salaried employees, especially young professionals and families
**Format:** 9:16 vertical animation
**Duration:** 35–45 seconds per episode
**Dialogue:** Natural conversational Malayalam
**Subtitles:** English
**Visual style:** Consistent, polished 2D cartoon animation

## SERIES OBJECTIVE

Help salaried people understand their spending, make a realistic monthly budget, control unnecessary expenses, and build a savings habit.

**Season challenge:** Manu tries to retain ₹3,000 from an illustrative ₹45,000 monthly salary over 30 days.

The numbers are examples for storytelling, not a recommendation for every viewer. Manu's budget must cover his actual obligations and essential needs.
