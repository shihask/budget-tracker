MONEY LEAKS

5 silent leaks

in your salary.

None of them feels like spending. All of them add up.

Which one is yours?Swipe
Leak 12 / 7
1

Subscriptions you forgot

An OTT plan, a music app, cloud storage, a gym you stopped going to. Small, monthly, invisible.

Fix: open your UPI autopay and card standing instructions once a month. Cancel anything you didn't use.
Leak 23 / 7
2

Paying only the card's minimum due

The unpaid balance carries over and collects interest. Credit card interest is usually higher than typical personal-loan or home-loan rates. Your statement shows your card's exact rate.

Fix: pay the total due, every month. If you can't, pause using the card until you can.
Leak 34 / 7
3

Small UPI payments

₹20 here, ₹80 there. Each one feels like nothing, so nobody counts them.

Small payments₹120 a day
× 30 days₹3,600 a month
Fix: log every payment for one week. Just seeing the total changes habits.
Leak 45 / 7
4

Late fees and penalties

A bill paid two days late, a cheque that bounced, a minimum balance you dipped under. Money spent on nothing.

Fix: list every due date in one place, and set reminders a few days before, not on the day.
Leak 56 / 7
5

"It's a sale" shopping

50% off something you weren't going to buy is not a saving. It's 50% of a new expense.

Fix: the 48-hour rule. Leave it in the cart for two days. If you still want it, buy it.

FAMILY BUDGET

The bills nobody plans for.

They don't come every month, so they always feel like a surprise. They aren't.

Save this before the next oneSwipe
The usual suspects2 / 6

List them for a year.

Health insurance₹18,000
School fees (terms)₹24,000
Festivals & gifts₹12,000
Vehicle insurance₹9,600
Vehicle service₹4,800
Total a year₹68,400

Example amounts. Yours will differ.

The trick3 / 6

Divide by 12.

₹68,400 a year ÷ 12₹5,700 a month

Treat that like a bill. Move it every salary day into a separate "yearly bills" account or pot.

When the bill comes4 / 6

The money is already there.

Vehicle insurance₹800 a month × 12₹9,600 ready
Festivals & gifts₹1,000 a month × 12₹12,000 ready

No credit card. No borrowing. No stress in festival month.

Getting started5 / 6

Start small.

1

Write down last year's surprises

Check your bank statement for the big ones.

2

Can't afford the full amount?

Start with the biggest bill, or half the amount. Anything is better than nothing.
